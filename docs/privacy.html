ReachPilot

Privacy

ReachPilot is a program you run on your own computer. There is no ReachPilot server and no ReachPilot account, so its author receives nothing from your copy: no posts, no statistics, no logins.

What stays on your computer

Your settings, drafts, posting history and the logins for the platforms you connect are kept in files next to the program. Those files are excluded from git, so they are not published when you push your copy.

The Google Search Console connection ("ReachPilot Search")

This connection is optional. It is a short Google Apps Script, findable_bridge.gs, that you paste into your own Google account and approve yourself. It asks for two permissions:

  • View and manage Search Console data for your verified sites. Used for two things only: to ask Google whether a page of yours is in its index, and to submit the address of your sitemap.
  • Connect to an external service. Used only to call Google's own Search Console service for the two things above.

The script answers only requests that carry a password you set, and it returns each answer to the ReachPilot running on your computer, which shows it on the Search page of your dashboard. The script stores nothing. The answers are not sent to the author or to anyone else, not sold, not used for advertising and not used to train any model.

ReachPilot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing it

  • Take back the permission at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the script at script.google.com.
  • Delete search_console from secrets.json in your ReachPilot folder.
